# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and reporters respectfully. Keep feedback focused on the work, explain disagreements and welcome questions. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted contact and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Participate only with data you may share. Do not publish credentials, private program details, sensitive target data or attack results in project discussions. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation. For a sensitive conduct concern, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without posting the details or identifying affected people.
